What this demonstrates

A practical bridge between demand, inventory policy, monthly production, distribution and line capacity. It is a streamlined public adaptation of the approach Felipe built before SAP implementation at WaterWipes. All sample business data is fictional.

Start with inputs

Open Guide in the workbook. Set product rates, pallet sizes, opening stock, target weeks and MTO/MTS policy in Inputs. Blue text marks editable inputs.

Enter demand

Demand contains 12 months, four products and two warehouse lanes per product. Edit forecasts, firm orders, confirmed inbound and signed production adjustments.

Review the plan

MPS rolls opening stock into demand, inbound, suggested and planned production, closing stock and weeks of cover. Current-month demand sets the cover target. MTO carries a zero stock target.

Discuss constraints

Capacity converts cases into required hours by product and sums the load by line. Red utilisation above 100% flags overload. The sample demonstrates a peak month that needs a planning decision.

Coordinate distribution

Distribution shows planned production allocated to each warehouse, converted to pallet equivalents. Production and delivery are assumed to occur in the same month.

Understand the boundaries

This edition consumes an entered forecast. It does not generate a statistical forecast, explode BOMs, schedule daily jobs, calculate changeovers or model quality release and transit lead times. Capacity is flagged rather than automatically constrained.

Adapt the structure

The sample is sized for four products and two lanes per product. To add products or lanes, extend and remap formulas as explained in Guide. It does not expand automatically.

Use the method

Run a planning cycle with your team. Identify exceptions, assign decisions and test improvements. The companion Site Deployment Playbook structures readiness, training, governance, escalation and sustained ownership.
